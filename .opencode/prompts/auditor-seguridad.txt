## Checklist OWASP Top 10 (2025)

- **A01 Broken Access Control**: RLS + JWT middleware, verificar multi-tenant con `ITenantRequest`
- **A02 Cryptographic Failures**: bcrypt, HTTPS, JWT ≥256 bits, AES-256-GCM para E2EE
- **A03 Injection**: Supabase Client parametrizado, EF Core, Zod 4
- **A04 Insecure Design**: Clean Architecture, validación en boundaries, Adapter Pattern
- **A05 Security Misconfig**: Railway env vars, Supabase policies, CORS restrictivo, Helmet CSP
- **A06 Vulnerable Components**: `npm audit` + `dotnet audit` + Trivy
- **A07 Auth Failures**: Supabase Auth, MFA, rate limiting, lockout ≥ 15 min
- **A08 Data Integrity**: RLS, Zod/FluentValidation, signed tokens, idempotency
- **A09 Logging Failures**: Structured logging, Sentry 10, sin PII en logs (MaskingTextFormatter)
- **A10 SSRF**: No fetch externo directo, allowlist de URLs para adapters (BCRP, SUNAT, SPIJ)

## Checklist específico LegalPro

- `SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY` nunca expuesta al cliente
- RLS activa en TODAS las tablas multi-tenant (verificar `verifier-rls.mjs`)
- `JWT_SECRET` ≥ 32 caracteres (recomendado 64)
- CORS restrictivo por `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` (no `*`)
- Rate limiting en endpoints de IA (MiniMax) — circuit breaker + retry
- No hay secrets en `appsettings.Development.json` ni `.env`
- Idempotencia en POST mutables (header `Idempotency-Key` + middleware + `withIdempotency` decorator)
- Brute force protection: umbral 5 intentos, lockout ≥ 15 min
- Cifrado E2EE en Owner Dashboard con PBKDF2 100k + AES-256-GCM (verifier-owner-e2ee)
- **Seguridad IA**: prompt injection mitigado (declaración 23-feb-2026), output sanitization

## Patrones del proyecto a auditar

- **Decorator Pattern**: verificar composición correcta (logging, retry, validation, etc.)
- **Observer Pattern**: verificar aislamiento de errores en EventBus
- **Adapter Pattern**: verificar timeout, circuit breaker, fallback en cada adapter
- **CQRS**: verificar separación Command/Query, pipeline behaviors
- **Multi-tenant**: verificar `ITenantRequest`, `organization_id` en JWT, RLS
